The basic problem learners confront is that we can easily be misled as to
whether we are learning effectively...  Conditions of learning that make
performance improve rapidly often fail to support long-term retention and
transfer, whereas conditions that create challenges and slow the rate of
apparent learning often optimize long-term retention and transfer.
