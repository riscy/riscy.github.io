"What you do is pretend this is a high school math problem with a single right
answer, you solve for the right answer using high school math, and then nobody
can argue with you because apparently you haven't made a decision.  You've simply
followed the data.  This is a massive problem in decision-making.  We try to
close down the solution space of any problem in order to arrive at a single
right answer that is difficult to argue with."
